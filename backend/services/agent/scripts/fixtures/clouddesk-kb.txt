CloudDesk Helpdesk Software
Product Knowledge Base & Support Guide

CloudDesk is a cloud-based helpdesk and ticketing platform for SaaS support teams.
It helps companies manage customer tickets, SLAs, knowledge articles, and AI-assisted replies.

## Core Features
- Omnichannel inbox for email, chat, and WhatsApp tickets
- SLA policies with business-hour calendars
- Knowledge base with versioned articles
- AI Copilot that drafts replies using approved articles only
- Role-based access for agents, leads, and admins
- Analytics for first response time, resolution time, and CSAT

## Pricing Plans
- Starter: 3 agents, 500 tickets/month, email support, $49/month
- Growth: 10 agents, 5,000 tickets/month, chat + WhatsApp, $149/month
- Scale: unlimited agents, custom ticket volume, SSO + audit logs, $399/month
Annual billing gives a 20% discount on all plans.

## SLA Targets
- Starter: first response in 8 business hours, resolution in 48 hours
- Growth: first response in 2 business hours, resolution in 24 hours
- Scale: first response in 30 minutes, resolution in 8 hours for P1 issues

## Integrations
CloudDesk integrates with Slack, Jira, HubSpot, Zendesk Migrate, and Stripe billing webhooks.
Webhooks can notify external systems when a ticket is created, assigned, or resolved.

## Security
- Data encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2+
- SOC 2 Type II controls are documented for enterprise customers
- Admins can enforce SSO via Google Workspace or Okta
- Ticket attachments are scanned and stored in isolated object storage

## AI Copilot Rules
- Answers must cite knowledge-base article IDs
- Copilot never invents refund policy details
- Refunds above $200 require lead approval
- Password reset links expire in 30 minutes
